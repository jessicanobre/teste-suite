Test Report 4 – Editing an Existing Contact
Title: Edit Contact Information
Description: This test verifies the ability to modify contact information.
Objective: To ensure edits to existing contacts are saved and reflected.
What is being tested: Update functionality in the Contacts module.
Prerequisites:

At least one contact must exist.

Logged in with edit permissions.

Test Procedure:

Go to Contacts > View Contacts.

Click on an existing contact.

Click “Edit” and change the email address.

Click “Save”.

Expected Result:
The contact’s new information is saved and visible.

Actual Result:
Changes were saved successfully and visible in the contact detail.

Result Analysis:
Test passed. Editing works as expected.
